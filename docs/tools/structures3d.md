## Space frames, plates and foundations (`structures3d`)

`structures3d_static`, `structures3d_modal`, `structures3d_buckling`, `structures3d_plate`, `structures3d_plate_stability`, and `structures3d_foundation` cover spatial frames, undamped modes, axial-force eigenbuckling, Kirchhoff plates, plate stability and bilateral Winkler beams. Unit-checked results include formulas, validity limits and CSV artifacts, with `review_status: "unreviewed"`. See [record formats, sources and verification](../structures3d/design.md).

A refusal that points into a record names the record kind and its number, shows the text received, lists the expected fields in order and gives an example, as in the `structures` domain.
